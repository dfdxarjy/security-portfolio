## What training developed

Across these topics, training strengthened structured problem-solving: breaking unfamiliar systems into understandable parts, recognizing security-relevant evidence, testing assumptions, and communicating findings with appropriate context. It also developed a broader view of how application, identity, network, endpoint, monitoring, and response concerns connect.

The value is not a single tool or technique. It is the habit of learning a system carefully, recording what supports a conclusion, separating observation from inference, and keeping defensive impact in view.
