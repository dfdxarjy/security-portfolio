## Credential

This page records my completion of **Hack The Box Pro Labs: Offshore**, issued
by Hack The Box.

## Why I took it

This Pro Lab was a chance to work on my own for a sustained period and to rely
on my own process rather than a guided structure. I wanted to strengthen the
discipline of documenting as I go, so that my conclusions stay tied to evidence
instead of memory.

## What it developed

The main takeaway was a more reliable way of working: slow the problem down,
write down what is actually known, and let the notes guide the next step. That
habit made it easier to stay calm when something did not work the first time
and to spot where my reasoning had drifted.

It also broadened my understanding of how application, identity, network, and
endpoint concerns relate — a perspective that matters on both sides of
security work.

## Reflection

The lesson I keep returning to is that progress came from consistency, not from
any flash of insight. Some stretches were slow and unglamorous, and those are
the ones that changed how I work. I came away with more humility about how much
there is to learn and more confidence that a disciplined approach scales.
