## Credential

This page records my completion of **Hack The Box Pro Labs: Zephyr**, issued by
Hack The Box.

## Why I took it

I worked through this Pro Lab to test how well I could hold a disciplined
approach together across a broad, loosely guided environment. I wanted to see
whether the habits I had been practising would hold up when the scope was
large and I had to set my own direction.

## What it developed

The experience reinforced a consistent method: reduce an unfamiliar situation
into parts I can reason about, record what I observe, and avoid treating an
assumption as a finding. It also built patience and a better sense of
prioritisation — knowing that chasing everything at once is rarely the right
move.

It gave me a wider view of how different security concerns overlap, which helps
me think about both attack and defence with more context.

## Reflection

Looking back, the value was in the routine rather than in any single moment.
Maintaining clear notes and honest reasoning over a long stretch was the real
challenge, and the parts that went slowly taught me the most. I am more
comfortable with uncertainty than I was, but I also see clearly how much depth
there is still to build.
