## Credential

**Hack The Box Mini Pro Labs: Mythical** — issued by Hack The Box.

## Why I took it

I worked through this mini lab as a structured way to practise holding a whole unfamiliar environment in
view rather than chasing isolated clues. The appeal was less the credential itself and more the discipline
of staying organised while the picture is still incomplete — deciding what to test next, and being honest
about what the evidence actually supports.

## What it developed

At a general level, the work reinforced habits that apply well beyond any single environment:

- Breaking an unfamiliar system into parts that can be reasoned about and checked one at a time.
- Keeping notes that separate what was observed from what was inferred, so conclusions can be revisited.
- Managing time deliberately instead of following the most interesting thread.
- Reviewing assumptions when progress stalls, rather than adding more noise.

## Reflection

The useful outcome was not a list of things I now know, but a clearer sense of how I work when the path is
not obvious. The most valuable moments were the ones that forced a pause: re-reading my own notes,
noticing an assumption I had stopped questioning, and choosing a smaller next step over a larger guess.
That habit is what I want to carry forward.
