## Credential

**Hack The Box Mini Pro Labs: Puppet** — issued by Hack The Box.

## Why I took it

I chose this mini lab as a deliberate test of method rather than a hunt for a specific answer. My goal was
to see whether I could stay methodical and evidence-led across a larger problem than the ones I was used
to, and to notice where my own habits held up and where they did not.

## What it developed

The exercise strengthened general working habits rather than any narrow trick:

- Structuring unfamiliar information so that gaps are visible instead of hidden.
- Writing things down as I go, so a later decision can be justified from the record.
- Distinguishing a confirmed observation from a working assumption.
- Keeping a steady pace instead of letting early momentum dictate the whole effort.

## Reflection

What stayed with me was how much of the effort was about management — of attention, of notes, and of my
own confidence when a problem did not resolve quickly. I finished with a better appreciation for patience
as a skill: the willingness to slow down, re-check the basics, and trust a careful method over a fast
guess.
