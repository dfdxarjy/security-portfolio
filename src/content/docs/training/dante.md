## Credential

This page records my completion of **Hack The Box Pro Labs: Dante**, issued by
Hack The Box.

## Why I took it

I chose this Pro Lab to move beyond isolated exercises and work in a setting
where progress depends on staying organised across a long stretch of
independent study. My aim was less about any single skill and more about
building a dependable way of working when the path ahead is not spelled out.

## What it developed

The clearest outcome was a stronger working method: approaching unfamiliar
systems methodically, keeping careful notes, and separating what the evidence
actually supports from what I merely assumed. It also strengthened persistence
and self-direction — deciding what to investigate next and recognising when to
change course.

More broadly, it deepened my appreciation of how offensive security and
defensive awareness connect. Understanding how systems can fail is part of
understanding how to protect them.

## Reflection

The hardest part was not any one task but sustaining disciplined judgement
across a large, open-ended environment. I finished with more respect for
documentation and for the difference between a hunch and a demonstrated result.
The credential marks progress in that habit.
